# Terms of use

**Pali Canon for Claude** · effective 1 November 2026

These terms explain how you may use this tool, in plain English. By using it, you agree to them. They apply to the software and to any official hosted version of it.

## What this tool is

A free, open-source library that helps AI assistants find and quote the Pāli Canon (the Theravāda scripture) and point to published English translations. It is **a library, not a teacher**: it gives you the texts and where they come from, not religious or spiritual instruction, and not professional advice of any kind.

## The texts are not ours

- Every text keeps its own licence. The bundled Pāli comes from the Vipassana Research Institute; translations come from dhammatalks.org and Access to Insight; dictionary entries come from the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary. Their terms are listed in [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md), and the tool returns the attribution and licence with every result.
- **Most of these licences are non-commercial.** Do not sell content retrieved with this tool, and do not build a paid product on it. Keep the attribution when you share a passage.
- AI assistants can still misquote or summarise wrongly. When it matters, check the passage against the source the tool names. A wrong citation or link is a bug: please [report it](https://github.com/DaniilKi/pali-canon-claude/issues/new/choose).

## Using the hosted version fairly

When an official hosted version runs, please:

- use it for reading and study, at a normal pace;
- not use it to copy the texts in bulk, to stress or attack the service, or to get around its limits;
- not try to make it reach websites other than the sources it is built for.

We may limit or block use that harms the service or the source websites. The hosted version is offered free and on a best-effort basis: it may be slow, change, or stop at any time, without notice. You can always run the tool on your own computer instead.
