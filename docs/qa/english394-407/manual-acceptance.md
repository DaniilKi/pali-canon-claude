scanners do not establish acceptance on real devices, by a screen-reader user,
or inside a signed-in assistant host. Record the exact reviewed commit and use
disposable local notes. Do not deploy or change production for these checks.

## Phone and tablet: eight minutes

On actual devices, record the model, OS, browser and version. Open Library →
Apadāna → reviewed publisher edition. Select Madhudāyaka (publisher394),
Girimānanda (397), Nanda (406) and Hemaka (407). Confirm the displayed publisher

and check that controls and words remain accessible. For Hemaka, follow the
actual Next control to finish the long English body. While it loads, type into
another input: late loading must not move focus or overwrite that input. The

disposable context. Check the exact passage, edition and note. An old389/390

## Human screen-reader session: five minutes

Use VoiceOver, TalkBack or NVDA with someone able to assess its output. Record
the actual browser and assistive technology versions. Check source selection,
heading/reading order, source and edition announcements, note controls, Next,
loading announcements, keyboard focus and the old-position review message.
Record actual failures; a clean automated scan is separate evidence.

## Signed-in assistant host: five minutes

Use the intended Codex, Claude or ChatGPT host and its actual connector. Request

## Record limits honestly

Record commit, device/host, versions, attempted checks and outcomes. Redact
account identifiers and personal notes. Loaded-page Saved behavior does not
establish first-run offline installation or an offline browser/PWA. Partial
