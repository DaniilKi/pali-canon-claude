## Additional scope hold: ATI AN 2.46 / indexed AN 2.47

The English *Ukkacita: Bombast* page matches the two assemblies' descriptions
but omits the final summary and explicit assertion that the questioning-trained
assembly is foremost. That is substantive content, not a repeated stock formula.
The omitted ending is inside `an2.47/2` (960 characters): summary at offset 828,
foremost assertion at 860. Both descriptions span the unit's two prose segments.
Selecting both as partial fails the proper-subset rule; selecting only the first
would falsely exclude English content from the second. No ordinary or partial
link is activated, and a regression pins that refusal against the actual unit.

This strengthens the case for the [separate condensed-page contract](ati-condensed-scope-review.md).
It does not justify bypassing its consent, page-level/null-ID quotation,
source/version/hash, coverage/export and full-consumer requirements. SN 36.15
also remains held. The feature is still a design, not a deployed capability.
