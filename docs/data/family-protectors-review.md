# Mother, Bright Protectors and the Itivuttaka scope repair (D201)

This review adds one condensed page and one alternate whole-discourse version,
and withdraws a mis-scoped collection binding. It does not create new scripture
or establish complete English coverage.

## Three decisions

**ATI Mother** is one [condensed page](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sn/sn15/sn15.014.than.html)
concerning SN15.14–19, VRI137–142: mother, father, brother, sister, son and
daughter. All12 stored root blocks establish that sequence. The publisher uses
common framing, ellipses and one conclusion. Six actual records are listed;
results retain `condensed:true`, false individual/whole-unit alignment and null
individual quotation IDs. Never reconstruct Pāli or split the page silently.
The separate dhammatalks Mother source remains excluded; reviewing ATI does not
review or authorize another source version.

**John D. Ireland's Bright Protectors** is a complete
[AN2.9 version](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/an/an02/an02.009.irel.html).
Both bright principles, the kinship-respect and animal contrast, and concluding
restatement match the complete Cariyasutta root, VRI9. AN2.8 only lists the two
qualities. The shared prose in Iti42 does not supply that text's framing and
verses. Preserve the already reviewed Ṭhānissaro AN2.9 version unchanged and
first. Both approved URLs fit the existing reviewed-link builder; no conflict
guard is relaxed. This adds a choice, not first-English coverage.

**Ireland's Group of Twos** at
`/tipitaka/kn/iti/iti.2.042-049x.irel.html` was attached as an ordinary Iti42
translation. It contains four distinct sections42,43,44,49 plus navigation.
Page-level wording from the other texts could therefore be wrongly attributed
to Iti42. It is globally withheld after every linking pass, regardless of owner,
query suffix or attached scope fields. Do not invent45–48 or treat the page as a
contiguous complete42–49 translation. No replacement scope is approved.
The existing valid dhammatalks Iti42 version remains selectable. Exact selection
of the withheld ATI version errors before fetch; it never substitutes DT.
