## Twenty whole-substantive numbered treatments

Every source paragraph was compared with its complete VRI root, including AN 1.20 and 1.41 as boundary controls. The first neighbour concerns attention and doubt; the latter contains the grain-awn simile and a wrongly directed mind. Neither belongs to this source selection.

The twenty treatments preserve both the comparison and the repeated mind assertion. They distinguish developed/undeveloped, apparent/unapparent, cultivated/uncultivated, tamed/untamed, guarded/unguarded, protected/unprotected and restrained/unrestrained conditions, with the correct result. Treatments 39–40 retain all four combined qualities. No teaching was found missing beyond the disclosed formula and paratext omissions: each source paragraph omits three `bhikkhave` vocatives and its root ordinal; root chapter headings in 21/31 and closing chapter colophons in 30/40 remain untranslated. Whole-substantive scope does not claim every word or word-level alignment.

The actual source contains exactly two chapter containers, each with ten bare paragraphs. Each starts with a real `<a id="sutta-N">N</a>.` label, followed by its own complete English treatment. There are no individual source headings, preface, notes, ellipses or merged pairs. Only the numeric label and its following layout spaces are removed from the returned scripture body. The printed label and actual parent title, *Ekadhamma Suttas: A Single Thing*, remain selection metadata; no individual publisher title is invented.
