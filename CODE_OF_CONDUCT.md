# Code of conduct

This project brings together people from many Buddhist traditions, scholars, monastics, translators, programmers and curious readers. We want it to be a welcoming, respectful place for all of them.

## Our standard

We adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as our code of conduct. Its full text applies to everyone who takes part in this project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

In short, we ask everyone to:

- be kind and patient, and assume good faith;
- respect different traditions, interpretations and levels of experience;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its readers;
- disagree about texts and translations with evidence (sources, page numbers, editions), never with personal attacks.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or demeaning comments, including about anyone's religion, tradition, ordination, gender, ethnicity, nationality or language; sexualised language or attention; publishing someone's private information; and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or monastic setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour:

- use GitHub's **"Report content"** option on the comment or issue, which reports it to GitHub; or
- [open an issue](https://github.com/DaniilKi/pali-canon-claude/issues/new) titled "Conduct report: please contact me", with no details, and a maintainer will arrange a private way to talk.

Reports are handled privately and fairly. The maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on what happened.
